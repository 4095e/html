About Mach24 Orbitals

Mach24 Orbitals stands at the forefront of Nepal's aerospace revolution, pioneering innovative satellite technology and space research solutions that position our nation as a credible player in the global space economy. Founded with the vision of advancing Nepal's presence in the cosmos, we specialize in custom satellite development for communication, earth observation, and scientific research applications, while providing expert consulting services on space missions and orbital mechanics to both government and private sector clients.

Our Mission

Our mission extends beyond technology development to encompass educational outreach and STEM initiatives designed to inspire the next generation of Nepalese aerospace engineers and scientists. We believe that by combining traditional Nepalese ingenuity with cutting-edge space technology, we can create solutions that benefit not only our nation but the entire global community. Through strategic partnerships with international space agencies, universities, and technology companies, we bring world-class expertise to Nepal while sharing our unique perspectives on sustainable space development.

Innovation and Research

At the heart of our operations lies a commitment to research and innovation, focusing on sustainable space technologies including debris mitigation strategies, efficient propulsion systems, and cost-effective launch solutions specifically tailored for emerging space nations like Nepal. Our current projects range from developing microsatellite constellations to establishing ground station infrastructure, all aimed at creating a robust domestic space industry that generates employment opportunities and drives technological advancement throughout the region.

Vision for the Future

We envision a future where Nepal plays a significant role in space exploration and scientific discovery, contributing valuable research while building economic prosperity through aerospace innovation. Our collaborative approach ensures that whether you are a student eager to learn, a researcher seeking partnerships, an investor looking for opportunities, or simply a space enthusiast passionate about the cosmos, there is a meaningful place for you in Nepal's extraordinary journey to the stars and beyond.
